A short checklist before you call

You don’t need to diagnose a plumbing problem yourself. A few practical notes can make the conversation more useful:

  • Write down when you first noticed the issue and whether it has happened before.
  • Identify the room or fixture involved, and say if other parts of the home are affected.
  • Note any visible signs, such as water where it shouldn’t be or a change in how a fixture works.
  • Keep the area accessible if it’s safe to do so. Don’t handle anything you’re unsure about.
  • Share any steps you’ve already taken, so the plumber knows what has and hasn’t changed.

Try not to take apart a fixture or use a product if you don’t know whether it’s appropriate. A quick explanation over the phone is more useful than a rushed repair attempt that could make it harder to see what’s going on.
